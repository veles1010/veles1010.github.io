Arrowword Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains how Arrowword handles gameplay information and how third-party services may process information.

Arrowword Support

Application / Developer

Arrowword is a mobile word puzzle application for Android and iOS, developed by veles10. It has no account system, user login, or developer-operated backend.

Information Stored on Your Device

Arrowword stores gameplay-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Current puzzle progress and completed puzzle information
  • Scores and puzzle history
  • Daily puzzle state and history
  • Settings, including language, theme, music, and sound-effect preferences

This locally stored gameplay data is not sent to an Arrowword developer-owned server: Arrowword has no developer-operated backend. Puzzle answers and clues are bundled with the app, and puzzle boards are generated locally.

Arrowword does not request or use device location, contacts, the camera, or the microphone. Ordinary gameplay does not require access to your photos or files. There is no app-owned analytics system or app-owned crash-reporting backend.

These statements about Arrowword's local gameplay data do not mean that third-party advertising services process no information.

Advertising

Arrowword includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) support for optional rewarded advertisements. You may choose to view an advertisement to reveal a puzzle letter. Watching advertisements is not required for ordinary puzzle gameplay.

Depending on the app version, advertising configuration, and applicable choices, Google and its advertising partners may process information associated with advertising, such as device identifiers, network information, app/ad interactions, and diagnostics. This information may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related advertising services.

Advertising and privacy options depend on the app's configuration, your region, device settings, and applicable consent choices. Personalized advertising is not necessarily enabled, and tracking permission is not necessarily requested.

For Google's practices and available controls, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

Internet Access

Core puzzles, locally bundled clues, and local progress do not require an internet connection. Connectivity may be required to load rewarded advertisements or open external support links. The advertising SDK may communicate with third-party services.

If an advertisement cannot load, ordinary puzzle gameplay remains available.

Sharing

For supported sharing features, Arrowword opens the device's native sharing interface only when you initiate sharing. You choose the destination application or service.

The information you choose to share is handled by that destination according to its own privacy practices. Arrowword does not control those third-party services.

Children's Privacy

Arrowword is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed toward children. If you have a question about a child's use of Arrowword or this policy, please use the Arrowword Support page.

Third-Party Services

Google Mobile Ads and its advertising partners have their own privacy practices. Applications and services selected through native sharing also operate independently of Arrowword.

If you choose to report a problem through the GitHub issues link on the Support page, GitHub processes the information you submit. Public issues may be visible to other people. Avoid posting personal or sensitive information.

Data Retention / Removal

Gameplay information and settings are kept locally on your device. You can generally remove local app data by clearing application data where your device supports it, or by uninstalling Arrowword.

Device or operating-system backup and restore features may retain or restore copies depending on your settings. Manage those copies through the relevant platform's settings.

The developer cannot remotely delete data stored on your device. Account-deletion requests are not applicable because Arrowword has no account system. Retention or removal of information processed by third-party advertising or sharing services is governed by those services' practices and controls.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Arrowword's features or relevant data practices change. The latest version will be available on this page with an updated date.

Contact / Support

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Arrowword, please use the Arrowword Support page.
