Arrowword Support

Help and information for Arrowword, the mobile word puzzle game from veles10.

Gameplay

Use localized clues and crossing letters to solve puzzles. Arrowword's answers are always English words using A–Z letters. You do not need an account or login to play.

Answers and Keyboard

  • Enter answers with Arrowword's built-in English A–Z keyboard and Backspace.
  • The language setting changes the interface and clues, not the language of the answers.
  • You do not need to change your device's keyboard language for ordinary on-screen play.

Rewarded Hints

You can choose to watch an optional rewarded advertisement to reveal a puzzle letter. Ad availability depends on the connection and advertising service.

If an ad is unavailable, you can continue normal gameplay without it.

Offline Use

Core puzzles work offline. Puzzle content and progress are kept on your device. Loading rewarded advertisements requires connectivity; opening external help links also requires internet access.

Audio

Music and Sound Effects can be enabled or disabled independently in Settings. Your device's media volume controls playback volume.

Privacy

Read the Arrowword Privacy Policy for information about local storage, optional advertising, sharing, and removing local app data.

Reporting a Problem

If you encounter a problem or have a question about Arrowword or its privacy policy, you can report it through the Arrowword GitHub issues page.

When possible, include the app version, device and operating-system version, and steps to reproduce the problem.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post passwords, personal information, or private files.
